\section{Preliminaries}
\label{sec:prelim}

Let $\mathcal{V}$ denote the vocabulary of an autoregressive LLM
$\mathcal{M}$, $V = |\mathcal{V}|$. At step $i$ the LM defines a
next-token distribution $p_i$ conditional on the prefix
$t_1 \ldots t_{i-1}$; its entropy is
$H_i = -\sum_t p_i(t) \log p_i(t)$. We assume throughout that the
verifier and the deployer share a secret key $k \in \{0,1\}^*$ and use
the same tokenizer. Let $H: \{0,1\}^* \to \{0,1\}^{256}$ be SHA-256,
modelled as a random oracle in the security analysis
(\cref{thm:security}). We write $[n] = \{1, \ldots, n\}$ and $z_\alpha$
for the one-sided standard-normal quantile at level $\alpha$.
