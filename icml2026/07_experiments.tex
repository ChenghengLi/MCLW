\section{Experimental Validation}
\label{sec:experiments}

We empirically test the Pareto and gate-invariance predictions of
Section~\ref{subsec:gates}, Theorem~\ref{thm:detection}, and Theorem~\ref{thm:quality} via a matched-budget
gate comparison on \texttt{google/gemma-3-270m-it} with $S{=}5$
clockwork MCL. The closed-form calibration of
Theorem~\ref{thm:detection} is derived analytically and tabulated in
Table~\ref{tab:cal}; we do not anchor it empirically.

\subsection{Protocol}
\label{subsec:protocol}

\textbf{Generator.} \texttt{google/gemma-3-270m-it} with
hard-partition MCL ($S{=}5$, clockwork). State assignment is
\mbox{$\sigma_k(t) = \mathrm{SHA\text{-}256}(k \,\Vert\, t) \bmod S$}
on the model's token IDs; the secret $k$ is fixed throughout.

\textbf{Prompts.} Two domains: \emph{Wiki} (173 concepts, prompts of
the form \emph{``Explain $X$ in a comprehensive way''}) and
\emph{Factual} (20 closed-form completions such as \emph{``The
capital of France is''}). Generation is greedy at gated positions
and uses temperature $T = 0.7$ at ungated positions; maximum 100
generated tokens per prompt.

\textbf{Gates.} We compare three gates from Definition~\ref{def:gate}
covering the watermarking design space:
$G_{H_{\text{high}}}$ (high-uncertainty positions),
$G_{\Delta}$ (near-tied top-2 positions), and
$G_{H_{\text{low}}}$ (low-entropy positions; the SWEET/EWD policy).
At each target budget $\rho \in \{0.25, 0.50, 0.75\}$, the
threshold $\tau$ of every gate is calibrated by quantile-matching
on a held-out pilot so the realised gate rate $\bar\rho$ tracks
$\rho$ within $\pm 0.03$. The full design has
$2 \,\text{domains} \times 3 \,\text{budgets} \times 3 \,\text{gates}
= 18$ cells; total wall-clock $\approx 3$~h on a single GPU.

\textbf{Per-text metrics.} We record perplexity (PPL, on the
generator's own logits), the fingerprint score $\phi$ and the
$z$-score from Algorithm~\ref{alg:detect}, and a binary detection
at $\alpha = 0.01$.

\textbf{Per-cell inference.} For each gate-pair within a cell, we
compute a per-prompt paired delta $\Delta_i = m_A(i) - m_B(i)$ and
a paired bias-corrected and accelerated (BCa) bootstrap 95\% CI
on $10{,}000$ resamples. We declare TOST equivalence at the
pre-registered margins
\mbox{$\Delta\log\text{PPL} \pm 0.05$},
\mbox{$\Delta z \pm 0.5$},
\mbox{$\Delta\text{TPR} \pm 0.05$}~\citep{lakens2017equivalence}.

\textbf{Attacks.} We perturb each watermarked output by
(i) random-token substitution at rate $\delta = 0.20$ and
(ii) WordNet synonym substitution at rate $\delta = 0.20$, and
re-run Algorithm~\ref{alg:detect} on the perturbed sequence.

\subsection{Non-watermarked baseline}
\label{subsec:baseline}

On the same generator and prompt sets, with no watermarking,
Algorithm~\ref{alg:detect} returns mean fingerprint $\phi = 0.181$
(predicted $1/S = 0.200$, within sampling noise) and mean
$z = -0.47$. This gives $\mathrm{FPR} = 0$ at $\alpha = 0.01$ on
$193/193$ samples. Mean perplexity on the unwatermarked baseline
is $\mathrm{PPL}_0 = 2.02$ (std $0.39$). The watermarked PPL
columns of Table~\ref{tab:cells} are therefore $1.5$--$16\times$
the unwatermarked baseline, with the multiplier set by the budget
$\rho$ and the gate.

\subsection{Pareto structure of the gate choice}
\label{subsec:pareto}

Figure~\ref{fig:pareto} establishes the headline empirical result:
$G_{H_{\text{low}}}$ is \emph{strictly Pareto-dominated} by both
$G_{H_{\text{high}}}$ and $G_{\Delta}$ on every $(\text{domain},
\rho)$ cell; the two non-dominated gates lie close to each other in
both PPL and $z$. The pattern holds at all three budgets and in both
domains, and matches Theorem~\ref{thm:quality}: the position-wise KL
$\log(1/Z_s)$ blows up where $Z_s$ is small, which is precisely the
low-entropy regime $G_{H_{\text{low}}}$ targets.

\subsection{Gate-invariance at matched budget}
\label{subsec:gate_inv}

Table~\ref{tab:equiv} reports paired BCa CIs and TOST verdicts for the
$G_{H_{\text{high}}}$ vs $G_{\Delta}$ contrast across the six
$(\text{domain}, \rho)$ cells. Three findings:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.3em, itemsep=1pt, topsep=1pt]
  \item \textbf{Quality is gate-invariant in point estimate.}
    The six $\log\text{PPL}$ point estimates lie in
    $[-0.21, +0.11]$; only the wiki $\rho{=}0.25$ CI sits inside the
    pre-registered $\pm 0.05$ TOST margin, so the equivalence is
    descriptive (point estimates near zero) rather than formal at
    $N{=}193$ pairs. The log-PPL TOST is structurally
    underpowered at this $N$; the conclusion is consistent with
    equivalence but not certified by it.
  \item \textbf{Detection probability is equivalent at
    $\rho \geq 0.5$.} Four of six cells formally pass the TOST
    $\Delta\text{TPR} \pm 0.05$ margin; both gates detect every
    watermarked text at $\rho \in \{0.5, 0.75\}$.
  \item \textbf{$z$ is equivalent at $\rho = 0.75$}
    (CI $[-0.07, +0.27]$). At lower budgets $G_{H_{\text{high}}}$
    has a small but reliable detection-strength advantage on wiki
    ($\rho{=}0.25$: $+0.46$ $[+0.15, +0.79]$;
    $\rho{=}0.50$: $+0.80$ $[+0.40, +1.21]$); the advantage lies
    inside the $\pm 0.5$ equivalence margin but outside zero.
\end{enumerate}
Operationally, the regulated dial is the \emph{budget} $\rho$, not
the gate identity; at $\rho \geq 0.5$ detection probability is
gate-invariant. At $\rho = 0.25$ TPR drops below $1$, with the
factual $G_{H_{\text{low}}}$ cell as low as $0.45$ (Table~\ref{tab:cells});
the practical regime is therefore $\rho \geq 0.5$.

\subsection{Theory--data consistency}
\label{subsec:theory_data}

Figure~\ref{fig:theory} cross-checks the closed-form predictions against
the same 18 cells.

\begin{figure*}[t]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.95\linewidth]{pictures/fig_theory.pdf}
  \caption{\textbf{Theory-vs-data match.} Gemma~3 270M, $S{=}5$,
    $n \approx 100$, 18 cells.
    \textbf{(A)} Predicted detection $z$ from Theorem~\ref{thm:detection}
    using the realised budget $\bar\rho$, versus observed $\bar z$.
    Points lie on or above the $y{=}x$ diagonal: the formula is a
    conservative lower bound, exceeded most by high-entropy gates at
    high budget where the gated argmax concentrates on the model's
    preferred successor.
    \textbf{(B)} Pre-attack $z$ versus post-attack $z$ at random
    substitution rate $\delta{=}0.20$. Theorem~\ref{thm:robustness}
    predicts $z_{\text{post}} = (1-\delta)^2 z_{\text{pre}} = 0.64\,
    z_{\text{pre}}$; every one of the 18 cells lies on this line to
    within $\pm 0.5$.}
  \label{fig:theory}
\end{figure*}

\textbf{Detection formula (Theorem~\ref{thm:detection}).} For wiki
$\rho{=}0.50$, $G_{H_{\text{high}}}$ ($\bar\rho = 0.53$, $n = 100$),
Theorem~\ref{thm:detection} predicts
$z = \bar\rho\sqrt{(S{-}1)(n{-}1)} = 10.55$; we observe
$\bar z = 12.71$. Across all 18 cells the observed $\bar z$
deviates from the prediction by $-0.4$ to $+3.8$ units, with
mean $+1.18$ and a one-sample $t$ of $3.13$
(Figure~\ref{fig:theory}A). The bias is structural, not symmetric:
$G_{H_{\text{high}}}$ and $G_{\Delta}$ at $\rho \geq 0.5$ overshoot
by $1.5$--$3.8$ because the gated argmax concentrates on
high-probability successors, lifting effective signal above the
hash-uniform null variance assumed by the formula. The formula is
therefore a \emph{conservative lower bound}, attained tightly at
low-entropy gates and exceeded at high-entropy gates.

\textbf{Robustness (Theorem~\ref{thm:robustness}).} Monte Carlo at
$10^{4}$ trials per configuration verifies
$\mathbb{E}[\phi \mid \text{attack}] = p_0 + \rho(1-\delta)^2(1-p_0)$
to within $0.01$ on $(S, \rho, \delta) \in \{(7,1,0),
(7,1,0.29), (5,0.5,0.2), (2,1,0.5)\}$. The universal midpoint
threshold $\delta^\star = 1 - 1/\sqrt{2}$ holds to $10^{-12}$ across
$(S, \rho)$. On live generations (Figure~\ref{fig:theory}B) the empirical
ratio $\bar z_{\text{post}}/\bar z_{\text{pre}}$ falls in $[0.63,
0.69]$ across all 18 cells, centred on the theoretical
$(1-\delta)^2 = 0.64$ within sampling noise.

\textbf{Self-healing (Theorem~\ref{thm:healing}).} Modified tokens receive
fresh random-oracle state assignments, so pairs touching an edit
contribute the null baseline $p_0$ in expectation; the watermark
signal survives in the unedited $(1-\delta)^2$ fraction of pairs.
Empirically, post-attack $\phi$ tracks the predicted mixture
$0.64\,\phi + 0.36\,p_0$ to within $\pm 0.03$ across all 18 cells.

\subsection{Takeaways}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.3em, itemsep=1pt, topsep=1pt]
  \item Low-entropy gating is strictly Pareto-dominated at matched
    budget, inverting the SWEET/EWD-inspired intuition.
  \item Quality and detection probability are gate-invariant at the
    practical region $\rho \geq 0.5$; the operational dial is the
    budget $\rho$.
  \item The detection formula and the universal $(1-\delta)^2$
    self-healing scaling agree with data on every cell to within
    finite-sample Bernoulli noise.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Out of empirical scope.} Theorem~\ref{thm:security}
(pseudorandomness) and Theorem~\ref{thm:hdd} (entropy-weighted
optimal detector) are theoretical only; the LM-access detector
$\Lambda$ of Theorem~\ref{thm:hdd} is not evaluated against $\phi$
here. Proposition~\ref{prop:kregular} ($k$-regular generalisation)
is verified at $k = 1$ (clockwork); empirical evaluation at $k \geq
2$ (e.g.\ soft-cycle) is left to future work.
