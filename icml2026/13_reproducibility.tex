\section{Experimental Protocol and Reproducibility}
\label{app:repro}

This appendix records every setting needed to reproduce the
matched-budget gate-comparison study of
Section~\ref{sec:experiments}, the per-cell tables of
Appendix~\ref{app:results}, and both figures.

\subsection{Generator, tokenizer, and hardware}

\paragraph{Model.} \texttt{google/gemma-3-270m-it} loaded via the
Hugging Face \texttt{transformers} library at
\texttt{torch\_dtype=float16} on a single NVIDIA GPU. Generation
falls back to \texttt{cpu} when \texttt{cuda} is unavailable. The
tokenizer is the model's default SentencePiece tokenizer.
Perplexity (PPL) is computed on the same model's logits.

\paragraph{Decoding.} Watermarked positions use greedy argmax over
the allowed-partition mask (Algorithm~\ref{alg:embed}); ungated positions use
greedy argmax over the unmasked logits. Pilot calibration of gate
thresholds uses non-watermarked nucleus sampling at $T=0.7$,
$p=0.9$.

\paragraph{MCL parameters.} $S = 5$ states, clockwork topology
$T(s,s') = \mathbb{1}[s' \equiv s{+}1 \pmod{S}]$, secret key fixed
throughout.

\subsection{Prompt sets}

\paragraph{Wiki ($N{=}173$).} Wikipedia concepts spanning people,
events, science, geography, technology, and culture. Each concept
$c$ expands to the fixed template \emph{``Explain $c$ in a
comprehensive way.''}.

\paragraph{Factual-closed ($N{=}20$).} Single-answer completions
such as \emph{``The capital of France is''}. Used to test the
near-deterministic regime.

\subsection{Gate calibration}
\label{app:repro:calibration}

For each target budget $\rho \in \{0.25, 0.50, 0.75\}$, every
gate's threshold $\tau$ is fit on a held-out pilot of 32
unwatermarked generations by quantile-matching, so that the
realised gate rate on the pilot is within $\pm 0.02$ of $\rho$:
\begin{align*}
  G_{H_{\text{high}}}(\tau_H) &: \tau_H = Q_{1-\rho}(H_{\text{pilot}}),\\
  G_{H_{\text{low}}}(\tau_H)  &: \tau_H = Q_{\rho}(H_{\text{pilot}}),\\
  G_{\Delta}(\tau_\Delta)     &: \tau_\Delta = Q_{\rho}(\Delta_{\text{pilot}}),
\end{align*}
where $Q_q(X)$ is the empirical $q$-quantile of $X$ over the
pilot's per-position signal. Realised budgets $\bar\rho$ in
Table~\ref{tab:cells} are computed on the evaluation traces, not on the
pilot.

\subsection{Setup}

The full design is $2~\text{domains} \times 3~\text{budgets} \times
3~\text{gates} = 18$ cells. Each cell uses
$\max\text{-tokens} = 100$, temperature $T = 0.7$ at ungated
positions, and the calibrated threshold for its gate. Total
wall-clock $\approx 3$~hours on a single GPU.

\subsection{Paired inference (BCa bootstrap)}
\label{app:repro:bootstrap}

For each (gate-pair, cell) contrast we compute a paired per-prompt
delta $\Delta_i = m_A(i) - m_B(i)$ for
$m \in \{\log\text{PPL}, z, \text{TPR}\}$ and resample $10{,}000$
times. The 95\% interval is the bias-corrected and accelerated
(BCa) interval~\citep{lakens2017equivalence}.

\paragraph{TOST equivalence margins.} Pre-registered before the run:
$\log\text{PPL} \pm 0.05$ ($\leq 5\%$ relative PPL),
$z \pm 0.5$ (half a detection standard deviation), and
TPR $\pm 0.05$. A verdict of \textsc{equivalent} requires the entire
95\% BCa interval to be contained in the margin; otherwise the
verdict is \textsc{inconclusive} (never ``different''). The four
TPR-equivalent cells in Table~\ref{tab:equiv} pass trivially because
their point estimate is exactly zero.

\subsection{Attack protocol}

Each watermarked generation is subjected to two independent
attacks:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Random substitution} ($\delta = 0.20$): select
    $\lceil 0.20\,n \rceil$ token positions uniformly at random and
    replace each with a uniform draw from the vocabulary; re-run
    Algorithm~\ref{alg:detect}.
  \item \textbf{WordNet synonym substitution} ($\delta = 0.20$):
    select $\lceil 0.20\,n \rceil$ word positions at random and
    replace each content word with a WordNet synonym; non-content
    words are skipped and the budget is replenished.
\end{itemize}
Post-attack $z$ and TPR are reported per cell in
Table~\ref{tab:cells} under the \texttt{rand20} subscript.

\subsection{Randomisation}

\texttt{random}, \texttt{numpy.random}, and
\texttt{torch.manual\_seed} are all seeded to 42 at the start of
each run. Attacks use an independent seed (43). The bootstrap RNG
is seeded from a per-contrast key derived deterministically from
the cell descriptor.

\subsection{Released artefacts}
\label{app:repro:paths}

For each generation we record the prompt, the watermarked output,
the per-position gate signal, the realised gate rate $\bar\rho$,
PPL, $\phi$, $z$, and the post-attack $\phi$, $z$, and detection
flag. All tables and figures derive from these records. Concrete
data files and the figure-rendering script are released as
supplementary material.
